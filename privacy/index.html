How we handle your data and the rules for using Web Highlighter.

Information We Collect

Only the data needed to make the extension work for you.

Account data (when you sign in)

  • Your Google account email (via Google OAuth)
  • Your display name and avatar URL (from your Google profile, if available)

Content you save

  • The text of each highlight you create
  • Any notes, tags, color choices, and notebook assignments you add
  • The URL and page title of the page you highlighted from
  • Timestamps (created, updated)
  • A DOM "anchor" that lets us re-paint your highlight on the original page

Sharing data (only if you use the share feature)

  • The email address you typed in when sharing a highlight
  • The permission level you granted (view or comment)
  • Comments and replies you write

Operational and product analytics data

  • Limited error reports via Sentry. Anonymized stack traces only. No highlight content, no URLs you visited.
  • For signed-in users, content-free product events such as panel opened, returned on a later day, selection started, save form opened, save started, save completed, save failed, save undone, and sync failed.
  • Product-event context is limited to whether the page used quote-only capture, the save mode, the destination type, and the extension version. Each event also has your account ID and a timestamp.
  • Product analytics never contains highlighted text, notes, tags, page titles, URLs, or hostnames. We use it to understand activation and reliability.

What We Don't Collect

  • We do not create a browsing-history log. On supported pages, the current URL is used to look up highlights you already saved for that URL. A URL, page title, and timestamp become saved content only when you save a highlight.
  • We read the selected passage when you choose to save it, and page structure when needed to restore highlights you previously saved. Page content is not sent to product analytics.
  • We do not track you across websites.
  • We do not run ads.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone, ever.
  • We do not use your highlights to train AI models.
  • We do not use cookies for tracking.

Data Storage

In two places:

  1. Supabase (PostgreSQL hosted in ap-south-1, Mumbai). Account data, highlights, notebooks, comments, shares, and content-free product events live here. The current page URL is also sent in a lookup query so the extension can restore highlights saved for that URL. Row-level security limits access to your account data.
  2. Your browser. chrome.storage.local caches your highlights for instant load. Cleared when you uninstall the extension or sign out.

Supabase is our database provider, subject to their own privacy policy (supabase.com/privacy).

Your Rights

  • Access. Your saved highlights, notes, tags, notebooks, and source details are visible in the side panel and can be exported anytime via Settings → Export. Email us to request a copy of other account-linked data.
  • Delete an account. Edit Profile → Delete Account. Permanently removes your highlights, notebooks, comments, shares, avatar, and product events from our servers. The deletion cascades through every related table.
  • Change your email. Edit Profile → Email change. Supabase emails a verification link to both your old and new addresses.

We comply with GDPR and CCPA. To exercise any right not covered by an in-app affordance, email support@webhighlighter.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Web Highlighter is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has signed up, email us and we'll delete the account.
